How to use Whapineo

Welcome to Whapineo! Below you'll find brief instructions on how to fully utilize this platform:

  • Search channels: Use the search box to find interesting WhatsApp channels by name or description.
  • Filter by country: Select a country from the dropdown to see channels only from that region.
  • Add channel: If you know a channel that's not on the list, use the "Add new channel" section and paste its link.
  • Rate channels: Click on the stars in the channel card to rate from 1 to 5. You can rate each channel once every 24 hours.
  • Add comments: Share your opinion about the channel by writing a comment in the text field and clicking "Add comment".
  • Join channel: Use the green "Join Channel" button to go directly to the channel on WhatsApp.
  • Boost channels: Click the "Boost" button to move a channel to the top of the list for 15 minutes!
  • Unboost channels: Click the "Unboost" button to move a channel to the bottom of the list. This action also has a cooldown.

We hope Whapineo helps you find amazing communities!
